I'm a Principal UX Designer with 12+ years turning complex enterprise systems — cloud consoles, banking platforms, supply chain, B2B commerce — into experiences operators actually understand. I work where domain-centred design meets user-centred design: pairing deeply with PMs and engineering on product strategy, owning end-to-end flows across multiple teams, and contributing to the design systems (HgDS / RDS at Oracle) that let those flows scale.

Before UX, I spent a year on technical support — most tickets came down to people drowning in a complex ecosystem. That problem is still the one I solve, just at a different altitude. Across Oracle, Deloitte Digital, Snowtint and Rage I've shipped for Banking (retail & B2B), Salesforce, Oil & Gas, E-commerce (Web / App / Kiosk) and Supply Chain — and led design partners through client and end-user research to ship product, not pixels.

Outside of shipping product, I mentor designers across teams, sit on our User Experience Review Board reviewing other services' work, and run a design publication documenting exploratory projects — a place for early-career designers to think out loud.

Let's build great products together.
