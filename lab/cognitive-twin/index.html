Cognitive Twin

A personal AI agent that runs entirely on your own machine.
Local-first Private by default A loved one's cloned voice Proactive, not static Open source · AGPL-3.0

Cognitive Twin is a digital version of someone you shape — or of someone you love. It learns who they are (a persona you create), can speak in their actual voice (cloned locally from a recording), remembers how you behave, reasons with a local model, and reaches out first. Nothing leaves your machine unless you allow it.

I started it for a personal reason: it can carry a loved one's warmth forward, in their own voice, on hardware you own. From there it grew into a broader idea — not a static assistant that only answers when asked, but something closer to an agent that knows you and thinks on its own.

It reaches out first

Open a chat and the twin speaks before you do — a time-aware hello in their voice, a thought it had while you were away, and a light nudge about your day. All from local context.

It keeps thinking while you're away

A background loop mulls the projects you actually keep raising and saves a fresh thought for next time — the “JARVIS keeps working” feel, entirely on-device.

Local models already handle a large share of everyday queries. The gap is the software around them — a persona, a voice, memory, and a loop that turns “do X” into real action, privately, on hardware you own.

How it works

  • Persona — an editable profile (name, traits, likes, values, style) compiled into the system prompt, so it reasons and speaks as that person.
  • Private memory — learns your recurring interests on-device; a single owner-only file, no telemetry.
  • Model routing — picks the right local model per request by policy (Ollama / LM Studio / Apple Intelligence / Unhosted).
  • Proactive loop — background reflection saves fresh thoughts between sessions; the chat opens with them.
  • Permissioned senses — web, screen, camera, and mic are each off by default and confirmed per use.
  • Runs everywhere — a shared Rust core compiles to macOS, iOS, Windows, Linux, Android, and the web.
Part of a bigger idea

The twin also merges into Unhosted — a local inference cluster that pools the hardware you already own. There, “Cognitive Twin” becomes a capability you simply enable.
